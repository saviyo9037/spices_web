IDUKKI SPICES — WEBSITE DESIGN ASSET PACK v2

Folders
- reference-designs/: Full-page generated website design mockups. Use as visual references, not as production page screenshots.
- shop-scenes/: Spice-shop / plantation visual references.
- product-assets/: Previously prepared cropped image pack for hero, categories, products, brand story, banners and recipes.
- video-previews/: Existing camera-pan / zoom motion previews.

Important notes
1. The full-page mockups are flattened images, not separate editable UI components.
2. The video previews were created from still images with simulated camera motion. They are not genuine multi-angle 3D walkthroughs, and individual products do not move independently.
3. Before production use, verify image licensing, product accuracy, actual product prices, stock, ratings, customer reviews, delivery promises, farmer claims, contact details and app links.
4. Do not use mockup prices/reviews as real data. Keep the existing project's API/backend as the source of truth.
5. Use WebP/AVIF derivatives for production and responsive image sizes where possible.
